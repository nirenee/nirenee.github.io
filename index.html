About Me

I'm Irene Onsurbe Casado, a Multimedia Engineer specialised in XR and game development with Unity, C#, Unreal Engine and C++.

I worked as a Gameplay Programmer (XR) at Hologate on multiplayer VR training simulations, and took part in the Develop at Ubisoft Mentorship Program, developing the roguelike Invoid in Unity. I've also worked at Niomake (facial animation engineer) and TinyFeetGames (sound design and engineer on Uprising: Humanum).

I love building immersive experiences and solving complex technical problems as part of a team.

I'm used working in international environments. I've studied in Milan, and worked in Lübeck and in Munich, and I truly value the perspectives that diverse teams bring.

INVOID

Invoid is a roguelike platformer developed in Unity as part of the Develop at Ubisoft mentorship program. Working under the guidance of industry professionals from Ubisoft, I contributed to the game's programming, focusing on gameplay systems and the core platforming and roguelike mechanics. The project gave me hands-on experience with professional development practices, iterative design and receiving feedback from experienced developers in a real studio context.

INTOEMOTIONS

IntoEmotions is a virtual reality experience designed to help teenagers develop their emotional intelligence. Developed as my Bachelor's thesis in Multimedia Engineering at the University of Alicante, the project uses immersive VR interactions to help players recognise, understand and manage emotions in an engaging, game-based way. Built entirely in Unity, I was responsible for all of the game's programming, from core gameplay systems and VR interactions to the overall game logic.
