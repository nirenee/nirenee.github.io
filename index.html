INVOID

Unity · C# · Develop at Ubisoft mentorship

Invoid is a roguelike platformer developed in Unity as part of the Develop at Ubisoft mentorship program. Working under the guidance of industry professionals from Ubisoft, I contributed to the game's programming, focusing on gameplay systems and the core platforming and roguelike mechanics. The project gave me hands-on experience with professional development practices, iterative design and receiving feedback from experienced developers in a real studio context.

Systems I programmed
  • Enemy AI: NavMesh-based state machine (patrol → chase) with a vision cone and raycast line-of-sight check, a wandering ranged enemy that stops to shoot, and a spawner that fills each level from a cost budget.
  • Procedural level generation: planets assembled from chunk prefabs joined by connectors, with weighted random selection from a ScriptableObject database, corridors matched by height, and the NavMesh rebaked at runtime.
  • Upgrade shop: data-driven items (ScriptableObjects) bought with gems, which boost the player's speed, damage, range, attack speed and health.

INTOEMOTIONS

Unity · C# · VR · Bachelor's thesis

IntoEmotions is a virtual reality experience designed to help teenagers develop their emotional intelligence. Developed as my Bachelor's thesis in Multimedia Engineering at the University of Alicante, the project uses immersive VR interactions to help players recognise, understand and manage emotions in an engaging, game-based way. Built entirely in Unity, I was responsible for all of the game's programming, from core gameplay systems and VR interactions to the overall game logic.

Systems I programmed
  • Core gameplay: the game logic behind the activities that ask players to recognise, understand and manage emotions.
  • VR interactions: all of the player's interactions with the immersive environment.

VR Burger

Unreal Engine 4.27 · Blueprints · OpenXR

A VR demo about making burgers, built in Unreal Engine with Blueprints. I was responsible for the programming and the user interface design, making the burger-building process intuitive and fun while keeping performance smooth in VR.

Systems I programmed
