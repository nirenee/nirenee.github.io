About Me

I am Irene Onsurbe Casado, a Multimedia Engineer with a diverse skill set in programming, game development, design, and web services. I’m excited to explore various opportunities in multimedia and game development.

Uprising Humanum

I was responsible for the design and implementation of the sound system for Uprising:Humanum using MetaSound in Unreal Engine. I worked on creating interactive audio experiences, ensuring the sound design enhanced gameplay, and programming dynamic audio responses to in-game events. My role involved collaborating closely with other team members to integrate sound seamlessly with the game’s mechanics, contributing to an immersive and engaging player experience.

Relic Raider

As part of a team of five students, I worked on a game called Relic Raider using our custom game engine. My main roles were composing and designing the music, creating textures, designing the user interface, and programming the enemy waypoints. It was a challenging project where we all collaborated to bring the game to life from scratch, and I had the chance to work on both the artistic and technical sides of development.
