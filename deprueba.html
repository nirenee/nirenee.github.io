About Me

I'm Irene Onsurbe Casado, a Multimedia Engineer specialised in XR and game development with Unity, C#, Unreal Engine and C++.

I worked as a Gameplay Programmer (XR) at Hologate on multiplayer VR training simulations, and took part in the Develop at Ubisoft Mentorship Program, developing the roguelike Invoid in Unity. I've also worked at Niomake (facial animation engineer) and TinyFeetGames (sound design and engineer on Uprising: Humanum).

I love building immersive experiences and solving complex technical problems as part of a team.

I'm used working in international environments. I've studied in Milan, and worked in Lübeck and in Munich, and I truly value the perspectives that diverse teams bring.
